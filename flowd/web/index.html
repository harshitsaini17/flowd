        Recognition

        Two local models: a fast one for the live preview, and a more accurate one for the text that gets pasted.

        Models

        Preview model
        Drives the words you see while speaking.
        Final transcript model
        Better accuracy on the pasted text. Adds about 1 GB of memory.

        Timing

        Pause before a phrase is committed
        Shorter commits sooner; too short splits phrases mid-thought.
        Trailing audio kept
        Extra audio after you stop, so the last word isn’t clipped.

        Voice-detection sensitivity has no effect with the current recognizer, which does its own segmentation (ADR 0002). It’s listed under Advanced.

        Apps & modes

        The mode shapes how cleanup writes. flowd picks it from the app you’re dictating into.

        Default
        Plain prose with sentence case and punctuation.
        Code
        Keeps identifiers and symbols, doesn’t add a closing period.
        Chat
        Casual. Short sentences, no forced capitals.
        Email
        Complete sentences and paragraphs.

          Privacy

          Nothing leaves this machine.

          Speech recognition and cleanup run locally. flowd makes no network requests, has no accounts and sends no telemetry. The cleanup server has to be on 127.0.0.1; any other address is rejected.

          Stored data

          Save recordings
          Keeps each dictation’s audio and transcript, for testing the recognizer on your voice.
          Every dictation’s audio and transcript will be saved as WAV and text in this folder until you turn this off.
          Log transcripts
          Adds your dictated text to the journal and the metrics log. Useful when debugging.
          Your dictated text will be written to the journal and metrics.jsonl. Anyone who can read your logs can read it.
