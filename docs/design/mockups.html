Indicator & popup

Every state of both desktop surfaces, rendered from flowd/web/assets/tokens.css. Specs live in design.md; the settings page is in settings.html. Turn blur off to see the no-blur fallback, which still meets contrast.

Live: one full dictation

Hover the line, then click it, or press Space while this page is focused (standing in for the global hotkey). Levels are simulated at 20 Hz.

Indicator states

A 140 × 48 layer surface with a pill inside. Input region: 120 × 20 when idle, the pill when expanded, empty while finishing.

Popup states

Polished (upright, full contrast), pending (dimmed, dotted underline) and live (italic, violet, caret). Max 4 lines, auto-scrolled, with a top fade when content is clipped.
